A Personal, Professional Approach to Quran Learning

Al Ameen Quran Institute is an online Quran learning platform founded and guided by Umair Amin. Our purpose is simple: to make authentic Quran education accessible to students of every age, anywhere in the world.

We believe that Quran learning is not a rushed activity — it is a lifelong relationship. That is why our classes are built on personal attention, consistency and clarity. Every student, whether a child or an adult, receives support tailored to their specific level and goals.

From learning the Arabic alphabet through Noorani Qaida, to building fluency and correct pronunciation, understanding Tajweed rules, and even working toward Quran memorization — our online classes support every stage of learning.

Our Mission

To make Quran education accessible, structured and meaningful through convenient online learning and personalized guidance.

Our Vision

To help students around the world develop a lasting connection with the Quran through consistent learning, understanding and practice.
