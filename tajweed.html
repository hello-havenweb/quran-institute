What Is Tajweed

Tajweed: The Art of Reciting the Quran Correctly

Tajweed refers to the rules that guide how each Arabic letter should be pronounced when reciting the Quran. It preserves the beauty and meaning of the words as they were revealed.

At Al Ameen Quran Institute, Tajweed is taught step by step with live guidance, so that every learner can build strong recitation with confidence.

Why Tajweed Matters

  • Preserves the correct meaning of words
  • Improves clarity and beauty of recitation
  • Builds confidence in reading aloud
  • Deepens focus and attention during recitation
What You'll Learn

Core Areas of Tajweed

◇

Correct Pronunciation

Pronouncing each letter clearly from its proper origin.

◇

Makharij (Articulation Points)

Understanding where each Arabic letter is produced in the mouth and throat.

◇

Sifat (Letter Characteristics)

Learning the qualities that distinguish each letter's sound.

◇

Madd (Elongation)

Applying correct lengthening in appropriate places.

◇

Noon & Meem Rules

Rules of Ikhfa, Idghaam, Iqlaab, and Izhaar.

◇

Waqf (Stopping)

Knowing where and how to pause during recitation.

Improving Your Recitation

  • Daily reading practice
  • Listening to correct recitation
  • Reciting aloud with feedback
  • Regular revision of learned rules
With a Teacher

Why Learning With a Teacher Matters

Tajweed cannot be mastered from books alone. It requires listening, repeating, and being corrected — which is why one-to-one live classes are the most effective way to progress.

At Al Ameen Quran Institute, every learner receives personal feedback in each session, ensuring steady improvement.
