Quran Courses

Comprehensive online Quran learning programs for all ages and levels

At Al Ameen Quran Institute, we offer a range of carefully designed Quran courses to meet the diverse needs of our students. Whether you are just beginning your Quran learning journey or seeking to perfect your recitation and memorization, we have the right program for you.

Quran Reading

Learn to read the Holy Quran from the basics of Arabic letters to fluent reading. This foundational course covers proper pronunciation, letter recognition, and building reading fluency step by step.

For: Complete beginners and those who want to improve basic reading
You'll Learn:
  • Arabic alphabet and letter recognition
  • Joining letters and forming words
  • Reading with correct pronunciation
  • Building fluency in Quran recitation

Quran with Tajweed

Master the art of beautiful Quran recitation by learning the rules of Tajweed. Understand proper articulation, pronunciation rules, and recite the Quran as it was revealed to the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

For: Students who can read and want to recite correctly with Tajweed
You'll Learn:
  • Makharij (articulation points)
  • Rules of Noon Sakinah and Tanween
  • Rules of Meem Sakinah
  • Madd (elongation) and other Tajweed principles
