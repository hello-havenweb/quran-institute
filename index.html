✦ About Us

About Al Ameen Quran Institute

Al Ameen Quran Institute is an online Quran learning platform founded and guided by Umair Amin. Our purpose is simple: to make authentic Quran education accessible to students of every age, anywhere in the world.

We focus on personalized, one-to-one learning so each student progresses at a pace that respects their individual level, needs and goals — from beginners taking their first steps in Noorani Qaida to those working toward Hifz.
