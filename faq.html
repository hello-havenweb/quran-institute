Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our online Quran classes, enrollment, and learning process.

What is Al Ameen Quran Institute?

Al Ameen Quran Institute is an online Quran learning platform founded and guided by Umair Amin, offering one-to-one classes to students worldwide.

Who can join?

Students of all ages and levels — children, teenagers, and adults — are welcome to enroll.

Do you teach children?

Yes. We offer age-appropriate Quran lessons for children with short sessions, encouragement, and parent communication.

Do you teach adults?

Yes. Our adult programs are respectful, private, and flexible — designed for beginners and improvers alike.

What courses are available?

We offer Quran Reading, Quran with Tajweed, Hifz / Memorization, Quran for Beginners, Quran for Children, Quran for Adults, Daily Quran Learning, and Islamic Studies.

Are classes one-to-one?

Yes. Our classes are designed as personal, one-to-one sessions for focused learning.

Can I choose my schedule?

Yes. You can choose your preferred days and times when enrolling.

How are online classes conducted?

Classes are held live online through Zoom at your scheduled time.

How do I join a Zoom class?

After enrollment, you will receive a Zoom link, Meeting ID, and Password. You can join from any device — computer, tablet, or phone.

How can I enroll?

You can enroll by contacting us via WhatsApp, email, or the enrollment form on our Contact page.

Still Have Questions?

Feel free to reach out — we are happy to help you with anything you need.
